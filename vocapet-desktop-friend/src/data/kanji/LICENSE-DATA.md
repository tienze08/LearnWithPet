# Kanji source data — licences

`graph.json` and `strokes/*.json` are **adaptations** of third-party data. They are generated by
`tools/importKanji.py` and must stay in this folder, apart from VocaPet's own content.

| File | Derived from | Licence | Holder |
|---|---|---|---|
| `graph.json` (strokes, grade, jlptOld, freq, radN, English meanings, on/kun readings) | KANJIDIC2 (database 2026-321, created 2026-11-17) | CC BY-SA 4.0, EDRDG General Dictionary Licence — https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html | James William Breen and the Electronic Dictionary Research & Development Group (EDRDG) |
| `graph.json` (krad) | KRADFILE / KRADFILE2 | CC BY-SA 4.0, EDRDG General Dictionary Licence | Michael Raine, James Breen and EDRDG |
| `graph.json` (parts, radC, usedIn), `strokes/*.json` | KanjiVG release r20260714 (`kanjivg-20260714-main.zip`) — http://kanjivg.tagaini.net | CC BY-SA 3.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/ | Ulrich Apel |

**Changes made:** only the listed fields were extracted for the kanji VocaPet teaches, all school/jōyō kanji of KANJIDIC2 grade 1-8, and their direct components, and reformatted as JSON. `usedIn` is computed from KanjiVG's component groups.
Stroke paths are copied unchanged from KanjiVG. The source file hashes are in `graph.json` → `meta`.

**Share-alike:** these files are distributed under the licences above. Do not relicense them.

**Acknowledgement in the app:** `/app/japanese/kanji-sources` (list in `src/lib/japanese/kanji-sources.ts`).

**Updating:** download newer source files, then
`python -X utf8 -I tools/importKanji.py <folder with kanjidic2.xml.gz, kradzip.zip, kanjivg-*-main.zip>`.

## Hán Việt (apart from the CC BY-SA files)

`../kanji-vi/hanviet.json` is generated by `tools/importHanViet.py` from Unicode Unihan (`kVietnamese`, plus
`kTraditionalVariant`, `kSimplifiedVariant`, `kZVariant`), Unicode License v3 (https://www.unicode.org/license.txt).
It is **not** CC BY-SA and is kept out of this folder. Hand-written Hán Việt from `src/lib/japanese/kanji.ts` always wins.
Re-run: `python -X utf8 -I tools/importHanViet.py <path to Unihan.zip>`; it also rewrites `docs/KANJI_HANVIET_REPORT.md`.
